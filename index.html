CASE BRIEFING

A Paper Out of Place

In this fictional case, a teacher took a question paper and its answer sheet for the Chongfu School 2026 Preliminary Examination from the staff room. The teacher secretly showed them to her favourite student, John.

The cupboard had been opened with keys. A pair of gloves had been used to avoid leaving fingerprints. Investigators have already confirmed the tools and location.

Your task: Identify the teacher and the subject of the stolen paper. Explain which clues support your answer.

Investigation rules

Exactly one of the four teachers took the paper. In this puzzle, each teacher teaches John only the subject listed in her profile, and the culprit took the paper for the subject she teaches.

Use evidence to identify the culprit. A possible motive or suspicious object is not proof on its own.

Clues & Evidence

Clue 1 · The language

The missing paper was written in English. At this school, the English, Mathematics and Science papers are written in English.

Clue 2 · A witness

A witness saw a teacher with long hair leaving the staff room at the time of the theft.

Clue 3 · John’s statement

“The paper I was shown was for my weakest subject.”

Clue 4 · The results

John scored higher in Mathematics than in English. He scored higher in Chinese than in Science. His Science score was lower than his English score.

Clue 5 · The broken keychain

A broken piece of a keychain John had given as a Teachers’ Day present was found near the cupboard. John had given a keychain to every teacher, so this clue alone cannot identify the culprit.

Clue 6 · Classroom observations

John behaved well in every lesson. His Science class was often rowdy. This may suggest a possible motive for favouritism, but it does not prove who stole the paper.

Detective’s Working Page

1. Find the weakest subject

Compare all three statements about John’s scores. You do not need his exact marks.

2. Rule out suspects

Tick every teacher you can rule out using the profiles and evidence.

Teacher Case Solution

Mdm Ng · Science · Keys and gloves · Staff room

Mathematics > English > Science, and Chinese > Science. Therefore, Science is lower than all three other subjects. John said he was shown his weakest subject, so the stolen paper was Science.

The rules state that the culprit took the paper for the subject she teaches. Mdm Ng teaches Science and has long hair, matching the witness’s description. A Science paper being written in English is consistent with the language clue.

The keychain does not identify anyone because John gave one to every teacher. The rowdy Science class offers a possible motive for favouritism, but does not establish guilt.
